Is it free?

Yes. This tool runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Do I need Shopify?

No. Unlike SoldLive/Dibsly/CommentSold, this needs no store, no integration, no monthly fee. Just paste and tally.

How do buyers claim?

However you already run it — “SOLD 3”, “mine 2”, or just the number. The tool reads all common patterns and you can edit before exporting.

Limited stock?

Turn on “First claim wins” and each item goes to the earliest commenter; later claims are flagged as waitlist.
